brouter.jar is BRouter 1.7.10, the routing engine by Arndt Brenschede and contributors
(https://github.com/abrensch/brouter), under the MIT licence in LICENSE.

It was compiled for Wikivault from the unmodified source of the release tag v1.7.10
(commit 4d2639af77ea5ed9c30d3e400764eb6f9e8522da, 17 July 2026) with:

  javac --release 11 -encoding UTF-8 \
    (all of src/main/java in brouter-util, brouter-codec, brouter-expressions,
     brouter-mapaccess, brouter-core and brouter-server)
  jar --create --file brouter.jar --main-class btools.server.RouteServer

The Android app and the map-creation tools are not part of it. It needs only the java.base module.

profiles2/ holds lookups.dat and four of BRouter's own routing profiles, unchanged:
car-vario, trekking, mtb and hiking-mountain.
