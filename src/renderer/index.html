          Wiki golf

          Get from one article to another using only the links inside the articles. Every article you open is a stroke, and the fewest strokes over the round wins.

          Take turns on this computer. Everyone plays the same hole, one after another, and the routes are shown once all have played. Search and Ask are switched off during a turn, and going back costs a stroke.

          Players

            Changing partitions normally goes without trouble, but a power cut or a disk fault partway through can damage a drive. Back up anything you cannot afford to lose before you continue.
