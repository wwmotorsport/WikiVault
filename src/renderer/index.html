                            Directions

                              Contacts accounts

                              The app reads your contacts and keeps a copy on this computer, so the addresses are here when there is no connection. It only reads: nothing in your account is ever changed. The copy is refreshed while the app is running.

                              Google

                              The simple way is an app password. Turn on 2-Step Verification for the Google Account, then make an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Google shows it as four groups of four letters, and it can be withdrawn there at any time. Google does not promise to keep this way open: if it is refused, use the sign-in further down.

                              The other way: sign in through a client of your own (longer to set up)

                              Google's official route for a program is a "client" registered with Google. This app cannot carry one for everybody, so you make your own, free.

                              1. Open console.cloud.google.com, sign in, and create a project (any name).
                              2. Under APIs and services, Library, find People API and press Enable.
                              3. Under Google Auth Platform, press Get started: give it any name, choose your own address as the support e-mail, choose External, give your address again as the contact, and press Create.
                              4. Under Audience, Test users, add your own Google address. The status stays Testing, in which Google ends the sign-in every seven days: you then press Sign in again here, and the contacts already copied stay meanwhile. Publishing the client, so that its status is In production, removes the limit, but Google first asks for a home page and a privacy policy page on a site of yours.
                              5. Under Clients, press Create client, type Desktop app. Copy the Client ID and the Client secret into the two boxes below.
                              6. Press Sign in with Google. Your browser opens Google's own sign-in page. It warns that the app is not verified, because the client is your own: choose Advanced, continue, and allow it to see your contacts.

                              Apple iCloud

                              Apple needs an app-specific password, not your usual one: make it at account.apple.com, under Sign-In and Security, App-Specific Passwords. It can be withdrawn there at any time. Two-factor authentication must be on for the Apple Account.

                              A file

                              A vCard (.vcf) file from any contacts program, or a CSV file exported from Google Contacts or Outlook. Importing again replaces what the last file brought in.

                              Sign-ins are kept encrypted by Windows for your user account on this computer, in the app's own data folder, and are sent only to Google or Apple. Contacts are not sent anywhere. The one exception is yours to allow: if you say yes to looking addresses up online, in the address book, the street addresses that the downloaded data cannot place (and nothing else about the contact) go to the United States Census Bureau's address service.
